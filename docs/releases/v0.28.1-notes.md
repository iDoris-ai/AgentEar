## v0.28.1 — Settings controls no longer overlap

**Recording, transcription, conversation, and the language settings behave as in v0.28.0.**
This release only rearranges the lower half of the Settings window.

### What this version does

On v0.28.0 the English labels “Speech recognition” and “Keep Raw Audio” were drawn across the full row, while the popups started partway across that same row. The words sat on top of “SenseVoice (built-in, default)” and “30 days”. The four buttons under them each took a full row.

The window is now 620 points wide. Those two captions sit on one row, each in its own half. The popups sit on the next row, each half as wide as the row. The four buttons are two rows of two:

- Edit Term List, and Open Voice Commands
- Open Data Folder, and View Log

Qwen3 download rows, the SenseVoice row, and the Agent24 row stay full width. The interface-language and recognition-language rows are unchanged.

### What an upgrade does

Nothing in the saved config changes. The next time Settings is opened, it uses the new arrangement.

### Verification

- `settings_columns_are_half_width_and_do_not_overlap` checks that the two columns are equal, about half the row, and do not cross.
- On 2026-10-27 a debug build was opened against an empty temporary data directory. The Settings window showed the two columns, the English button titles fit, and the focus ring was not left on the first popup. That process was stopped with SIGTERM. It was not the packaged `.app`.

### Limits

- The signed `.app` in this release was not clicked. Opening that bundle would rewrite the login item.
- Chinese and Thai labels were not re-shot in this pass. They were already shorter than the English labels that overlapped.

## v0.28.1 —— 设置窗口下半区不再重叠

**录音、转写、对话和语言设置都和 v0.28.0 一样。**
这一版只重排设置窗口的下半部分。

### 这一版做了什么

v0.28.0 里，英文 “Speech recognition” 和 “Keep Raw Audio” 占满一整行，下拉框又从同一行的中段画起，字压在 “SenseVoice (built-in, default)” 和 “30 days” 上。下面四个按钮各占一整行。

窗口现在宽 620 点。这两个标题同一行、各占一半。下拉框在下一行，各占半行。四个按钮排成两行：

- 编辑术语表，和打开语音指令
- 打开数据目录，和查看日志

Qwen3 下载行、SenseVoice 行、Agent24 行仍是整行。界面语言和识别语言那两行没动。

### 升级会发生什么

已保存的配置不变。下次打开设置就是新排法。

### 验证

- `settings_columns_are_half_width_and_do_not_overlap` 钉住两列等宽、大约各占半行、互不交叉。
- 2026-10-27 用调试构建对着空的临时数据目录打开过设置窗口。两列都在，英文按钮标题放得下，焦点环没有留在第一个下拉框上。该进程用 SIGTERM 停掉。那不是打好的 `.app`。

### 边界

- 这一版签名包里的 `.app` 没有点过。打开那个 bundle 会改写开机启动项。
- 这一轮没有重拍中文和泰文界面。重叠出在更长的英文标题上。
